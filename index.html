How To Play

1. Pick a pack. Choose any trivia topic from Select Trivia Pack, or build your own in Create Trivia Pack.

2. Set up teams. Add 2–8 teams with names and colors.

3. Play the board. Teams pick a category and dollar value. Reveal the question, then Reveal Answer, then mark it Correct or Incorrect for the buzzing team — scores update automatically.

4. Watch for Daily Doubles. A random cell lets the team wager before seeing the question.

5. Finish with Final Jeopardy. Every team wagers, then answers the same question for the win.

Keyboard shortcuts: Arrow keys to navigate, Enter to select, Space to reveal, Esc to close, F for fullscreen, R to reset the board.

About Trivia Night Pro

Trivia Night Pro is a reusable, browser-based game-show trivia platform. Build unlimited trivia packs on any topic, run classic Jeopardy-style boards, track team scores, and play entirely offline once loaded — no server or account required.
