Teams pick a category and dollar value. Reveal the question, then Reveal Answer, then mark it Correct or Incorrect for the buzzing team — scores update automatically. 4. Watch for Daily Doubles. Keyboard shortcuts: Arrow keys to navigate, Enter to select, Space to reveal, Esc to close, F for fullscreen, R to reset the board.
